Games for the 8x8 display


1. Dodge
   
   Move Left (4 Key) and Right (6 Key) to avoid the oncoming blocks.
   The rows speed up the lonher time rolls on.
   
   Left most display is the number of lives left.
   Right most display is the total number of rows dodged.
   
   When all you lives are lost 'X' is displayed. Press a button to restart.

1. Snake
   
   The rules are simple, eat the food but don't let the head of the snake
   touch any part of the body.
   
   Press a direction key to start.
   1 = UP
   9 = DOWN
   4 = LEFT
   6 = RIGHT
   
   Left most display is the number of lives left.
   Right most display is total food consumed.
   
   When all you lives are lost 'X' is displayed. Press a button to restart.
   

1. Breakout
   Remove the bricks by bouncing the ball into the bricks.
   
   Key 4 moves the paddle left, key 6 moves the paddle right.
   Key 5 launches the ball.
   
   Left most display is the number of lives left.
   Right most display is number of bricks removed.
   When all you lives are lost 'X' is displayed. Press a button to restart.
